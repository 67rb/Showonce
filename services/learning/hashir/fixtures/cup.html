Paper cup practice

Authored paraphrase fixture based on Origami.me cup tutorial; not a website snapshot.

Step 1

White side up: fold the diamond bottom to top.

Step 2

Bring the upper left edge to the base; mark only the right endpoint.

Step 3

Fold the left tip to that mark.

Step 4

Fold the right tip across to the left tip.

Step 5

Lower the front top flap to the rim.

Step 6

Fold the remaining top triangle behind the rim.

Step 7

Spread the opening and flatten the base.
